# Overview

The Krista Salesforce Marketing Module Extension connects to the Salesforce system and its Marketing module and performs various operations.

### Prerequisite
* To connect to the Salesforce Marketing system, you must have a Salesforce Developer account.

### Note:

The Krista Salesforce Marketing Module Extension:
  * Supports only the **Campaigns** entity.
  * Does not support any catalog requests.
